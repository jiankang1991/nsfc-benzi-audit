## Choose The Applicable Version First

Record application year, exact category, form version/source, funding mode and submission stage before a compliance finding. A historical funded example retains its own year. Do not apply the latest rules retroactively to an old submission or describe the 2026 baseline as confirmed 2027 requirements.

For an unknown year or unavailable official template, continue content diagnosis and mark form/compliance status 未核查. Do not demand legacy headings, budgets, signatures or attribute fields from incomplete extracts. Resolve apparent conflicts by source date, scope and effective provisions.

## Review Process And Diagnosis Boundaries

The 2026 青年 C类 management rules specify scientific value, novelty, social impact and plan feasibility, considering innovation potential (Article 14). They require at least three effective correspondence reviews and a majority of attending panel members for a funding recommendation (Articles 15–16). Inspect other categories separately. [Official rules](https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/p1/2871/2874/2882/136725.html)

Do not assign universal percentages to novelty, prior work or team composition. Do not predict funding from an A/B label, reviewer workload or a supposed acceptance band. Assess title/abstract clarity because readers need to understand the proposal; do not assume panels read only those parts. Publication type, authorship position and preliminary results are contextual evidence, not universal automatic rejection criteria.

Useful substantive checks include unsupported novelty, unclear questions, infeasible routes, unexplained overlap with past work, outcomes unsupported by the plan, and material citation/terminology errors. Report concrete evidence rather than an alleged official ranked rejection list.

## Other Rule-Sensitive Surfaces

- **Eligibility and limits:** verify category, year, applicant status, relevant ongoing projects and required attachments from official documents. Do not infer qualification from publication count.
- **Codes and keywords:** match object, methods and contribution to the current code description. Historical project distributions suggest alternatives; they do not determine the code or identify individual reviewers. Use the specificity required by the target form rather than universal code lengths or keyword rankings.
- **Integrity and representative works:** check author order, personal contribution and the source of preliminary results. Actual contribution matters more than a title or venue label. Consult the current notice for attachment and contribution-statement requirements.
- **Ethics, safety and data:** inspect relevant human/animal, biosafety, data, geographic/sensitive-data and collaboration requirements using applicable rules. An attachment absent from body extraction is a material gap until the attachment set is known.
- **AI assistance:** the 2026 notice requires human verification of AI-assisted information/references, responsibility for used content, truthful disclosure and relevant labeling; directly AI-generated applications and unverified generated content are prohibited. Keep this skill's output as diagnosis and fact-conditional revision skeletons. This report does not itself establish the applicant's disclosure compliance. Formal expert review has a separate scope and requirements. [2026 notice, I(二)8](https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/p1/3381/2824/99667.html)

Use official sources for binding requirements: [annual application regulations](https://www.nsfc.gov.cn/p1/2931/3971/3974/sqgd2.html), the relevant project notice and the actual system template. FAQs are navigation aids; prefer more specific binding documents/forms where available. Record title, publication/effective date when available, relevant provision and access date.
