# Scientific-Question Distillation

Use for question provenance, rationale convergence, bottlenecks and originality/interdisciplinary arguments. Historical inspiration comes from the case book《凝练科学问题案例》and its expert commentaries, recorded in earlier repository revisions. These are writing/argument observations, not official rules or proof that a wording caused funding. Claim burdens are maintained in [research-claims.md](research-claims.md); verdicts follow [benzi-logic.md](benzi-logic.md).

## Question Provenance

The historical six-mode taxonomy is a way to recognize sources, not a classification gate:

| Possible source | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| 好奇引导 | The unexplained observation or relation and why it matters |
| 无穷追问 | What an existing result leaves open |
| 反常深挖 | The anomaly and evidence that it is not a trivial artifact |
| 范式变革 | What changed in theory, data or instruments to make the question tractable |
| 同行之议 | The actual unresolved question behind a discussion or consensus |
| 透视需求 | The scientific relation behind an application obstacle |

Literature, theory, public data, own observations and demand can each motivate a question. Evaluate the source the author actually uses. Personal preliminary discovery, a technology window, meeting history or a prescribed sequence is not a universal prerequisite.

For an anomaly used as the principal premise, inspect relevant alternative explanations and supporting source evidence. A published systematic analysis may already supply it; exploratory uncertainty may be part of the proposed study. Do not manufacture an exclusion sentence or require the applicant to have completed the decisive research.

## From Source To A Researchable Question

A useful chain is existing knowledge/observation → unresolved relation → difficulty → proposed approach → expected knowledge. It can be written in different orders and across sections. Check whether the reader can reconstruct it from the supplied material.

- Identify the object, conditions and unknown relation. A noun phrase, interrogative or model/method term can express a valid question; inspect its actual explanation.
- Distinguish scientific contribution from a deliverable label. “Build a model” needs a property or knowledge question; estimation validity, generalization and evaluation can provide one without a mechanism claim.
- Link questions through their contribution to the project; serial dependence is optional. Use the core COV map rather than count or paragraph format.
- Separate source evidence from applicant readiness. A literature-motivated theory question can be supported by relevant mathematical capability and resources.
- A question can have a narrow but useful result. Ask for significance only where its importance is not explained; do not demand an unsupported universal extension.

## Originality, Bottlenecks And Interdisciplinary Claims

Current binary research attributes and historical four-category scientific-question labels are different fields; their formal applicability belongs to [current-rules.md](current-rules.md). On any form, inspect actual claims using the following aids:

| Argument | Useful diagnostic question | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 原创 | What knowledge or method property is added relative to relevant prior work? | No prerequisite of a published impossibility or existing external endorsement; use NOVEL |
| 独辟蹊径 | Under comparable conditions, what does the alternative route make possible? | A useful new route need not prove every existing route futile |
| 需求牵引/瓶颈 | Which obstacle becomes a researchable relation, and how is its importance evidenced? | Quantitative performance/limit claims need comparable quantities; not every conceptual or theoretical gap is numerical |
| 交叉融通 | Why is the combination needed, and what does it contribute? | No mandatory bidirectional benefit, “dimension jump” phrase or fixed form of cross-disciplinary output; use TRANSFER when validity is borrowed |

When the draft alleges an existing theory is inapplicable, identify the relevant assumption and the condition said to violate it. When it alleges a physical limit, distinguish a principled limit from current implementation or resource constraints using MEASURE. These are conditional burdens arising from the claim, not new sections every proposal must add.

## Difficulties And Supporting Arguments

Useful difficulties include a relevant method's failure under the target condition, ambiguity, complexity, scale differences and resource limits that affect validity. Match proposed innovations to the difficulty they address. A resource shortage can matter to feasibility without being promoted into a scientific problem.

Organization, meetings or team size can support execution; they do not by themselves demonstrate a scientific increment. A method contribution may change assumptions, a bound, representation, inference procedure or evaluation principle. Do not dismiss it solely because an empirical improvement is also reported.

For proxies, scale transfer and analogy, use TRANSFER. For selective/specific effects or mechanisms, inspect the evidence that discriminates the target claim from alternatives using CAUSAL/PRED as appropriate. For a new metric, inspect the property it is meant to measure and its proposed validity test; first-principles reconstruction is not mandatory for every metric study.

Prefer validation conditions that can distinguish the proposed claim and its alternatives. An especially demanding scene, a known/unknown pair or a two-column analogy table may help, but none is a universal requirement. Likewise, an unexplained choice among plausible routes calls for its actual scientific/resource rationale, not an exhaustive rejection of every imaginable alternative.

## Reporting

Name the missing relation or unsupported premise, cite its source locations and run the core countercheck. An argument already present elsewhere may need only a cross-reference. Group the same issue under one finding ID; optional writing suggestions do not become separate scientific defects. Do not assign reviewer scores or infer substance from praise, hedging or funded-example vocabulary.
