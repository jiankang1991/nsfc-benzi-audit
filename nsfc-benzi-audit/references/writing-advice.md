# Optional Writing And Navigation Advice

Use for requested polishing or when expression makes an otherwise supported argument difficult to follow. These are writing aids, not official requirements or sufficient grounds for a 必改 finding. The substantive decision stays in [benzi-logic.md](benzi-logic.md).

## Title And Abstract

A useful title exposes the object, question and a meaningful condition or contribution. A restrictive modifier should be explained where it matters in the body. Do not enforce a noun-phrase format, a length band, a punctuation ban or inclusion of every logic element in one title. Remove repetition or self-evaluation when it obscures the actual study; a word alone does not establish an unsupported scientific claim.

The abstract can move through object/problem → method/goal → proposed work/contribution → expected knowledge/significance. These are informational roles, not fixed paragraphs or one sentence per task. Follow the actual form's length and formatting requirements. For a verified 400-character limit, 80/80/180/40 = 380 is one illustrative allocation, not a quota for each part.

Possible skeleton, only after the applicant verifies its facts:

> 针对[对象及条件]下的[明确问题]，拟通过[方法/分析路径]研究[关系或性质]，形成[可检验结果及适用边界]，为[具体知识或应用判断]提供依据。

Do not insert an applicant's preliminary discovery when the question actually comes from literature or theory. Include numbers only when needed to convey the scope or quantitative claim; not every abstract needs parameter ranges.

## Rationale And Question Expression

Help the reader connect existing knowledge, its relevant limitation, the proposed question and the expected increment. Chronological, geographic or thematic subsections can all work if they synthesize that connection. A closing paragraph, comparison table or original overview figure may help; no particular device is mandatory.

Questions may use interrogatives, noun phrases or prose. Read the explanation beneath a title before labeling it a task. “建立模型” may introduce a real question about identifiability, validity or generalization; make that relation visible where the current text hides it.

For significance, connect the knowledge result to its scientific or practical use. A narrow result can matter; do not demand an unsupported universal extension, a fixed ordering of scientific/application value or a national-strategy slogan.

## Tasks, Innovation And Prior Basis

Show what is studied, the desired result, the route and validation without enforcing separate legacy headings. Linear dependency, complementary studies and feedback loops are all possible architectures. Tables or topic sentences can expose relationships already present; their absence alone is not a missing scientific connection.

For innovation, clarify the difference from relevant prior work under comparable conditions. Distinguish planned results from completed components using the actual surrounding text; tense alone is not proof the whole project is already done.

For research basis, a useful sentence is “已有[可核对工作]支持[具体步骤]，在[新条件]下仍需解决[真实剩余问题]”. It does not require a matching publication for each task or a prescribed declaration that the applicant is changing direction.

## Reduce Reader Work

Suggest clearer terminology, shorter repeated background, a meaningful topic sentence or a selective diagram when it resolves an observed reading difficulty. Do not prescribe boldface, a figure count, a list length or a reviewer reading-time theory.

In the diagnosis report, group expression suggestions briefly after substantive findings. Refer back to one issue ID instead of repeating its explanation under title, rationale, innovation and consistency. If no useful expression change follows from the actual material and request, omit this surface.
